Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 24, 2026

Meal Daily Planner ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

Meal Daily Planner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data remains on your device.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Family member names and preferences (adults/children)
  • Allergy information you provide
  • Available ingredients you enter
  • Subscription status and trial start date

This data is stored using Apple's UserDefaults and is never transmitted off your device.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external service used is Apple's StoreKit for processing in-app subscriptions, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Subscription management is handled by Apple.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Family member names entered are stored locally and never shared.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
