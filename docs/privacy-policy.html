Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 12, 2026

Meal Daily Planner ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults:

  • Family member names and adult/child status
  • Allergy and dietary restriction information you provide
  • Available ingredients you enter
  • Meal preferences text you write
  • Nutrition targets and body metrics you enter (if configured)
  • Subscription status and trial start date
  • Daily meal plan (cached for the current day)

Data Sent to AI Service for Meal Plan Generation

When generating your daily meal plan, the app sends the following information to our secure backend server, which forwards it to OpenAI's API to produce your personalised meal plan:

  • Family member first names and whether each is an adult or child
  • Allergy and dietary restriction information
  • Available ingredient preferences
  • Meal preferences text you have entered
  • The current date (for meal variety)

This data is used solely to generate your meal plan. It is not stored, logged, sold, or used for any other purpose by us or our backend. No user account, email address, device identifier, or location is ever transmitted.

OpenAI processes this data according to their own privacy policy: openai.com/privacy

If the device has no internet connection, meal plans are generated entirely on-device and no data is sent anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following external services:

  • Apple StoreKit — for processing in-app subscriptions. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI API — for AI meal plan generation (see section above). Governed by OpenAI's privacy policy.

The app does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Subscription management is handled by Apple.

Children's Privacy

The app is designed for family use and allows adding child family members by first name. Child names may be included in meal plan generation requests sent to OpenAI (as described above) solely to tailor portion sizes. No other information about children is collected or transmitted. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 beyond what is described in this policy.

Data Deletion

All locally stored data can be deleted by uninstalling the app. Data sent to OpenAI for meal plan generation is not retained by us after the response is received.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
